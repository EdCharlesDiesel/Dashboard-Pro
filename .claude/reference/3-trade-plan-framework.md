# 3 — Trade Plan Framework

**Source: `.claude/skills/experienced-institutional-fx-trade/SKILL.md`.**
That skill is the full framework; this is the output contract every trade
thesis on this desk must satisfy.

## The five headers, in order

### 1. Macro View & Big Picture
Risk-on or risk-off, central bank leaning, the data on the calendar. Sets the
stage before any level is quoted.

### 2. Asset Analysis & Trade Thesis
Fundamental and technical, plus positioning. **State conviction: High / Medium
/ Low.** A thesis without a stated conviction cannot be sized honestly.

### 3. Trade Setup
- **Entry** — a level or a range
- **Stop** — ATR-based, or a named structural level
- **Target** — minimum **1:2** risk-reward
- **Position size** — % of account at risk (0.5–1% standard, never above 2%)
- **Counter-indicators** — what would invalidate this

### 4. Recommended Execution Strategy
Spread in pips, and the order type it implies: spread > 0.5 pips → limit inside
the spread; very tight spread on a major → marketable limit, where speed is
worth more than the fraction of a pip.

### 5. Execution Cadence & Contingency
How the position is entered, and the conditions that pause or abort it.
"If the 4H closes back inside the range, cancel." "If CPI beats, stop to
breakeven immediately."

## Non-negotiable

- **Risk-first.** Downside before upside, every time.
- **Minimum 1:2 R:R.** Below that the win rate has to be implausible to break even.
- **Every plan names its invalidation.** A plan without a counter-indicator is
  not a plan, it is a hope with numbers attached.
- **Benchmark against the arrival price** — the mid when the trade was first
  discussed. That is what execution is measured against, not the day's low.
- **If live data is missing, ask for it.** Do not estimate a spread, an ATR or
  a balance to keep the narrative moving. Stop and ask.

## Tiering by grade

| Grade | What it earns |
|---|---|
| **A**, quality gate passed | Full five-header plan, full risk budget |
| **B**, quality gate passed | Full plan, half risk budget |
| **A/B**, quality gate failed | Written up, **not traded** — say which quality criterion failed |
| **C / D** | Not written up. Note it and move on |
